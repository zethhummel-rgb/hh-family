Google Calendar, with a space for home.

The household owner can choose Connect Google Calendar in HH. With permission, HH creates a dedicated secondary calendar in that Google account, copies existing HH calendar events into it and uses it for the household calendar.

HH can read, create, edit and delete events in that app-created calendar to show plans, synchronize changes, support repeating events and deliver enabled reminders. Members of the HH household can manage those events through the app. Google Calendar sharing controls who else can see them in Google.

The connection uses calendar.app.created, rather than access to all of your existing Google calendars. Read how we handle Google Calendar data before connecting.

Your own home starts with an invitation.

Open the invitation link, make an account and confirm your email. A new-household invitation lets you create your own home; a family-member invitation joins the household that invited you. Google Calendar is an optional connection made by the household owner.
