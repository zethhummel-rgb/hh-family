HH Family is a shared home organizer for family plans, groceries, to-dos and recurring reminders. Each person has their own login. Each household has its own private space.

Family calendar

See the month or an agenda, tag family members, choose colours and find past or upcoming plans. Open an event to see its details before editing.

Shared groceries

To-dos & reminders

Share tasks, set recurring reminders and keep completed items separate. Device notifications are optional and require permission on each supported device.

Your HH calendar, with an optional Google copy.

Your shared plans and reminders live in HH. The household owner can connect Google to see a copy in a dedicated family calendar. Make changes in HH; edits in Google do not change HH. If Google needs reconnecting, your ready HH calendar keeps working. Existing Google-connected homes are preserved and verified before their calendar source changes.

HH requests access only to calendars created by the app. Your other personal and work calendars stay separate. Read about Google access and privacy.

Your own home starts with an invitation.

Open the invitation link, make an account and confirm your email. A new-household invitation lets you create your own home; a family-member invitation joins the household that invited you. Google Calendar is an optional connection made by the household owner.

HH is currently being tested. Google access is limited to approved test users while the Google app remains in Testing mode.
