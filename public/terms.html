Terms of service

Effective October 19, 2026 · HH Family invitation-only pilot

These terms apply to HH Family (HH), a household organizer pilot operated by the HH pilot administrator at hummelhomeapp@gmail.com. By using HH, you agree to these terms and acknowledge the Privacy policy.

The pilot

HH offers a shared household calendar, groceries, to-dos and reminders to invited users. It is an experimental pilot: features may change and service or notifications may be interrupted. Access to the current pilot is free; any future paid offering would require separate notice and agreement.

Your account and household

Use an email account you control, provide accurate account information and protect your credentials. An adult household owner should manage invitations, profiles and any optional Google connection. Only add or share information you have permission to use, including information about family members.

Household members can view and change shared content. Share invitation links with intended recipients only. A new-household invitation creates a separate household; a family-member invitation joins an existing household.

Your content and Google Calendar

You retain your rights to content you add. You allow HH and its service providers to process that content as needed to operate the features you use, as described in the Privacy policy.

Connecting Google authorizes HH to create a dedicated calendar, copy existing HH events into it and manage events on that calendar for your household. Edits and deletions may synchronize between HH and Google. Google provides its services under its own terms, and Google Calendar sharing settings may allow others to see events. You can revoke HH's Google access in your Google Account.

Responsible use

Do not use HH to access another household without permission, harass others, upload unlawful content, send spam or interfere with the service. Do not attempt to obtain other users' credentials or bypass access controls. Pilot access may be restricted to address abuse, security concerns or the end of the pilot.

Reminders and availability

Calendar synchronization and notifications depend on Google, Supabase, network connectivity and device permissions/settings. Delivery and continuous availability are not guaranteed. Keep another record or alarm for anything where a missed reminder could cause harm or a serious loss.

Ending use and changes

You may stop using HH at any time. For account or household deletion, contact hummelhomeapp@gmail.com; we may verify your identity and authority. Revoking Google access or deleting your HH account does not automatically delete the dedicated Google calendar or all shared household content. See the Privacy policy for details.

Material changes to these terms will be communicated through the app or the account contact address before applying to continued use where required. These terms do not exclude rights you have under applicable law.
