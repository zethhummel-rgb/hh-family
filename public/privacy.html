Privacy policy

HH Family invitation-only pilot · Updated November 1, 2026

This policy describes the HH Family web app (HH), operated by the HH pilot administrator, reachable at hummelhomeapp@gmail.com. HH helps invited households share calendar events, groceries, to-dos, reminders, family notes and meal plans.

Information HH handles

  • Accounts: your email address, display name, login/session information and selected calendar colour. Supabase Auth handles password authentication; HH does not display your password to other household members.
  • Household information: household name, membership and roles, invitation email addresses, invitation status and email delivery attempts, and family profiles you choose to add, including names and colours. Profiles may represent family members without their own accounts.
  • Shared content: grocery items, optional quantities/units and store names, and saved grocery staples; family note titles, text, pin status and creation/update times; planned meal dates and sections (breakfast, lunch, supper or snacks) and saved meal titles, ingredients and optional ingredient quantities/units, cooking notes, source links and creation/update times; to-dos, due dates, recurrence and completion information, and calendar event details such as title, times, location, description, tagged people, reminder preferences and Home countdown choices.
  • Optional notifications: device push subscription endpoints and encryption keys, permission/subscription preferences, and notification delivery records needed to send reminders and avoid duplicate deliveries.
  • Technical records: hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, request times, browser/device information, errors and security logs to run and protect the service.

Google Calendar access and use

Google Calendar is optional. HH offers an ongoing Google copy and a separate one-time import. Only a household owner can start or reconnect the ongoing copy. That feature requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created permission and uses a dedicated secondary calendar created for the household.

HH’s built-in calendar is the main record of household plans. Connecting Google creates an optional copy in the dedicated Google calendar. HH sends event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, recurrence, family tags and reminder preferences to keep that copy updated. Additions, edits and deletions in HH are queued for Google; if its authorization expires, HH’s calendar and reminders continue working while the copy waits. Changes made directly in Google are not automatically imported into HH.

For a household that already used Google as its HH calendar, the existing calendar remains active until a one-time transfer is preserved and verified. HH reads that dedicated calendar, including recurring events, changed or cancelled occurrences and related metadata. It keeps a private server-side snapshot of the Google data and any earlier HH records so the transfer can be checked and recovered if needed. The source changes only after a successful transfer; if it cannot finish, the existing calendar remains active. These preservation records are inaccessible to the browser and are removed with the household or its stored Google connection. This automatic transfer covers only the dedicated household calendar. Importing another calendar requires the separate consent and selection described below.

HH stores the dedicated calendar identifier, the connecting account’s HH user identifier, connection/copy status and the household event records. An ongoing Google authorization token is stored encrypted on the server to update the optional copy while the owner is away. HH’s reminder service reads HH events once the verified transfer is complete; existing Google-connected households continue using their existing source during the transfer. Copy work and delivery records contain the identifiers and limited status information needed for retries, deletions and duplicate prevention.

Import an existing Google calendar: A signed-in household member can separately authorize a one-time import. HH requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly to list calendars and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly to read events. These permissions allow reading calendars available to that Google account; HH reads event details from the calendar you select for preview. The import does not edit the selected Google calendar or send invitations to event guests.

The preview includes event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations and supported recurrence details. You choose which supported events or series to copy, their household person tags and whether their automatic 30-minute notifications are enabled. You can skip tagging to add calendar-only plans without assigning people or sending notifications. Imported events become shared HH records visible to every member of your household; person tags do not make an event private. If your household has enabled an optional Google copy, the new HH records are also copied there. Later changes in the original Google calendar do not update the imported HH records, and HH edits do not update the original calendar.

Import authorization is stored encrypted on the server for a short session; HH does not keep an ongoing refresh token for imports. Temporary previews are restricted to the importing account and household and expire automatically. Cancel or finish the import to clear the temporary authorization and preview. HH retains source-calendar and event identifiers with the household to prevent repeat imports, including after an imported HH event is deleted. Imported HH content and duplicate-prevention records are removed when the household is deleted. You can revoke Google's permission in your Google Account; clearing HH's temporary import session does not itself revoke that permission.

Google user data is used only to provide these household calendar and reminder features. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train general-purpose AI models. HH's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

For a household whose owner requests a move from the original Hummel Home, a temporary server connection can keep that household’s existing calendar and lists available in both apps. It is restricted to the designated household, keeps sign-in accounts and Google credentials separate, and remains in place until the final transfer is verified. Private preservation snapshots retain the original records for the move. Device notification permissions remain separate for each app.

Feedback you choose to send

Send Feedback emails your chosen HH area, comment, account email, display name and household name to hummelhomeapp@gmail.com. Your account email is included as the reply address. Feedback and delivery status are stored privately for support and duplicate prevention, and are not shared with other household members. These database records are removed when your account or home is deleted; copies already delivered to the support mailbox are retained separately for support until no longer needed or an applicable deletion request is completed. Please do not include passwords or sensitive family information.

Who can see information

Household members can view and manage shared household content, including the calendar, family notes and meal plans, through HH. Members can read, edit, pin and delete their household’s shared notes. All household members can also view and manage meal plans and saved meals. A household's records are restricted to its members; an invitation to create a different household does not grant access to yours. The household owner manages invitations and family profiles.

Google Calendar permissions may independently make the dedicated calendar or its events visible to people you share it with in Google. An enabled device notification may display an event or task title on a lock screen, depending on device settings.

The pilot administrator has administrative access needed to operate the service. Human access to Google data is limited to your affirmative permission for specific support, security or bug investigations, or legal requirements, consistent with Google's Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides account authentication, the household database, server functions and server-side secret storage.
  • Google provides a household's connected calendar and Gmail delivery of invitation, account confirmation and password-reset emails. Invitation emails contain the recipient address, inviter and household names where applicable, expiry and an email-bound invitation link. Account emails contain the recipient address and relevant account links.
  • GitHub Pages serves HH's public app files and information pages.
  • Your browser or operating system's push provider delivers notifications when you enable them.

These providers process information needed to supply their services under their own terms and privacy policies. Provider processing may occur in different countries. HH does not add advertising trackers or sell household information.

Household updates: When someone changes shared household content, HH keeps a short unread summary for the other current members, including the member’s display name, the action, item title and time. Each member can open these updates from Home; viewing the list clears the displayed summaries for that account, across its devices. Other members’ unread lists are separate. Unread summaries stop appearing after 30 days and are cleaned from active storage on the next household change. Removing a membership clears its unread inbox; deleting a household removes its update summaries. This is not a permanent activity history.

Feedback

When you choose Send Feedback, HH sends your selected area, comment and account email to hummelhomeapp@gmail.com so we can respond. The delivery process also uses your household and account identifiers, request identifier and delivery status to verify access and avoid duplicate submissions. Leave passwords and sensitive family details out of comments. Feedback is separate from shared household notes. Sending is disabled in the Hummel Home test environment.

Browser storage and protection

HH uses browser storage for sign-in, invitation flow, appearance and the last open screen, separately for each account and household. After the grocery list has loaded, HH also saves grocery records and queued grocery additions, edits and checkmarks on that device for offline shopping. These records can remain while you are signed in. Changes stay on the device until they are accepted by the server or you resolve a conflict. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted by the device; open HH online before shopping and verify pending changes have synced before clearing site data or signing out. Signing out clears the associated local shopping copy. Unsaved form drafts are not permanently stored; closing a changed form asks you to keep editing or discard it. Installing the app also caches app files. Household access is enforced on the server; Google credentials remain server-side.

With Stay signed in, HH can keep a sign-in on your device between visits. The limited pilot account switcher can keep two separately authenticated sign-ins on that device, or just for the current tab when Stay signed in is off. Switching does not combine household information or grant additional permissions. HH does not save the passwords you enter in the switcher. Use saved sign-ins only on a device you trust, and use Sign out of both accounts before sharing it.

No service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account and device secure, share invitation links only with intended recipients, and avoid entering information your household does not need to share.

Your choices, retention and deletion

You can edit or delete shared items, family notes, meals and events in the app, change your colour/preferences and turn off device notifications. Deleting an event removes it from the active HH calendar and queues deletion of its Google copy, where connected. That copy is removed when Google access is available.

Planning a saved meal makes a separate copy for that date. Editing or deleting the saved meal does not change already-planned meals. Removing a meal plan does not remove its saved meal or ingredients already added to groceries. Shared meals remain when one member leaves and are removed when the household is deleted. Source links are optional; opening one takes you to another website, whose privacy policy applies.

You can revoke HH's Google access in your Google Account's third-party connections. Revoking access stops further authorized calendar access; it does not itself delete the calendar, existing events or HH household records. You can manage or delete the dedicated calendar in Google Calendar.

To request account deletion, removal of the stored Google connection, a copy of your information, or deletion of a household you own, email hummelhomeapp@gmail.com from your account address. We may verify your identity and household authority before acting. Leaving or deleting one member's account does not by itself erase shared content belonging to the remaining household.

Deleting your account or home in Settings: A household member can delete their own login account from Settings → Your profile by typing their email address to confirm. This removes their membership, login account and sessions, while shared household content remains for the other members. A linked family profile remains as a profile without a login. A household owner can instead use Settings → Your household → Delete home and all accounts, typing the exact household name to confirm. This permanently removes the home’s HH data and every member’s login account and sessions, including the owner’s. Members lose access to that home. Pilot administrator accounts and households are protected from self-service deletion. These actions cannot be undone through the app and do not send a deletion email. Deleting a home removes HH’s Google connection and encrypted credential, but does not delete calendars or events already stored in Google. Provider logs and backup copies remain subject to the retention processes described below.

Administrator household deletion: The authorized pilot administrator can permanently delete an entire household through the admin-only Manage households screen in Settings, after typing the exact household name to confirm. This removes the household’s shared HH calendar records, groceries, saved staples, family notes, meal plans, saved meals, to-dos, reminders, family profiles and memberships, along with all household members’ login accounts and sessions. It also removes HH’s stored Google connection and encrypted authorization credential. This action cannot be undone through the app and does not send a deletion email. It does not delete the dedicated calendar or events stored in Google; those must be managed separately in Google Calendar. The administrator’s own household is protected from this deletion control. Provider logs and backup copies remain subject to the retention processes described below.

Account and household records are retained while used to provide the pilot, until removed through the app or an authorized deletion request, or as necessary for security and legal obligations. Operational logs and provider backups follow the applicable providers' retention and deletion processes; deletion from the active app does not necessarily immediately remove every backup copy.

Family profiles and changes

The pilot is intended to be set up and managed by adult household owners. Adults adding information about children or other family members should have authority to do so and limit it to what is needed for household organization.

We will update this policy when practices change. A material change to how Google data is used will be disclosed before using it in the new way, with new consent where required. Contact hummelhomeapp@gmail.com with privacy questions.
