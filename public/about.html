Using Google Calendar with HH

Your household owner can connect a dedicated Google calendar to see a copy of your HH plans. Make changes in HH to keep the copy up to date. HH requests access only to the calendar it creates. Your personal and work calendars stay separate. Google access is limited to approved users while the Google app is in Testing mode.
